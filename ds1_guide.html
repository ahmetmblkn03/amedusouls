    • Overview

    Dark Souls Remastered is a fantasy role-playing game released in 2018 by FromSoftware Inc and is a remaster of Dark Souls. It is well renowned and infamous for being difficult and unforgiving in nature. For new players to the series, this game may prove quite a challenge with its inherent difficulty. Compared to the later titles Dark Souls II: Scholar of the First Sin & Dark Souls III, it is much slower and clunkier than those games, and at times more forgiving.

    What is this guide? This guide aims to create an easier and streamlined pathway to acquiring the Platinum Trophy. This guide was made for Dark Souls Remastered; however, you may use this guide for the original release of Dark Souls. The walkthrough provided in this guide is for strictly Offline play, but you may trade with other players through boosting sessions through this site.

    What lies ahead? If you are not a fan of collecting items, there is a plethora of things to do that are tied to trophies. The majority of these items are missable, so consider any item colorized missable, or potentially missable if you don't clean up prior to finishing a playthrough. There is minimal grinding required compared to the original Dark Souls and the other 2 titles, as Item Discovery is much more friendly in this game.

    The difficulty of this guide is based on the guide author's suggestion to a new player to the series, who follows this guide and utilizes the Soul Duplication Glitch. This difficulty may be higher or lower depending on your personal skill level. If you have the help of other players through summoning, the difficulty will decrease even further. If there are any tips or strategies not provided in this guide that will help you, please feel free to use those in lieu to suit your own playstyle.

    Gameplay Information

    Dark Souls Remastered can be very uninformative when it comes to how to actually play the game. You are presented with a screen when you begin the game with all of the buttons for controls, and that's basically it. Along the way you may find messages on the ground, prompts to perform certain actions, but nothing is really informing you.

    Let's begin with the controls of this game. As this is the first title in the trilogy, there is nothing fancy compared to the other two titles. If you want to attack, you can press a button and it will happen. You want to power attack, that happens with a button press as well. There are some other niche attacks and combos with certain weapons, but for the purposes of acquiring the Platinum trophy, the weapons are simple to use.

    Bonfires are a unique mechanic to the Dark Souls series and they give the player the ability to rest, repair Weapons & Equipment, attune Sorceries, Pyromancies or Miracles to spell slots and Reverse Hollowing. With the discovery of certain items explained in this walkthrough, you may also upgrade your Weapons & Equipment at the bonfire, which is unique to Dark Souls & Dark Souls Remastered.
    • In addition to the above, at a point in the story you will gain the ability to Warp to specific bonfires in Lordran. This is the only Dark Souls game that warping is not unlocked from the beginning of the game, which increases the difficulty slightly.
    • Resting at a bonfire resets 99% of all common enemies in all areas. There are some enemies that do not respawn, such as Black Knights and Crystal Lizards. You can use this mechanic to farm certain areas such as Darkroot Garden if you choose not to use the Soul Duplication Glitch.
    • Do keep in mind that if any enemies follow you close to the bonfire, you will not be able to rest. You will also not be able to rest if you have summoned an Ally, or if you have been invaded by a Dark Spirit.

    Soul Duplication Glitch

    There are numerous glitches that can be performed in Dark Souls Remastered that can aid you in completing the game easier, with less bosses or can flat-out break the game. For simplicity, this guide will only utilise the Souls Duplication Glitch, which enables you to consume 999 of a soul item, or other consumable that does not require a confirmation.

    The way this glitch works is it bugs out the menu to make the game think you are using 999 units of an item, when in fact you are only using 1. By exploiting the arrows and consumables menu, you can overlay the settings menu to trick the game. It is very easy to setup and use, and will expected during this walkthrough. The video below explains it in detail how to get the glitch to work.




    This glitch is mainly going to be used with Souls Items and Humanity Items during this walkthrough. It will not work with Boss Souls or with the Fire Keeper Soul. Using this glitch will allow your character to gain very high levels early on and hit the soft caps for all attributes mid game. If there are any other glitches you wish to perform that are not included, please feel free to experiment and enjoy the game how you want to.

    • Approach the fog gate at the top of the path and equip the Divine Claymore+5 and Crown of Dusk.

    • Cast Fall Control to get things started and enter the fog gate. Drop down into the chasm below and watch the cutscene with Gravelord Nito. Once this cutscene is over, the boss has the ability to attack you within seconds, so beware.
    • The main attack to be concerned about is his Gravelord Dance. You will hear a woman's scream coming from Nito just before he strikes his weapon into the ground. Once you see the blade connect with the ground, roll to the left or right as a giant sword will come out of the ground.
    • Now that this attack is explained, shield up and take out the first three Skeletons with the Divine Claymore+5. They will remain dead once you kill them. Try not to advance further into the area as there are Giant Skeletons ahead.
    • From a distance, cast Pursuers and cast Crystal Soul Spear to kill him off quickly. You may supplement the magic with Dark Bead if you want to kill him with less spells.
    • Gravelord Nito has some dangerous attacks which are explained below. There will be no shield guarding as he will just hit straight through it.
      • He can slash and stab with his sword, which will inflict Toxic to you if hit multiple times. Roll out of the way of this attack when you see him raise or pull the sword back to his sides.
      • If you see him bow down, you must run away from him. He will cause a massive shockwave which will inflict large damage, break your Stamina and cause toxic if you fail to get away.
      • If you get close to him he has the ability to grab you, which can be negated by spamming  + , or just stay away from him.

    Part 11: Dark Sun Gwyndolin, the Darkmoon God

    This section of the walkthrough will be quite short. You will join the final covenant for this playthrough, Blade of the Darkmoon and acquire the remaining Miracles that can be acquired as well. The remainder of cleanup will be performed during the next portion of the walkthrough.

    • Equip the Hawk Ring and the Black Bow of Pharis+15 for the upcoming boss fight. You may equip any great bow if you have the ammo for it, making this boss fight go by quicker.
    • Please read the below strategy on how to easily defeat Dark Sun Gwyndolin. This boss will warp frequently, so an exploit will be used.




    • Dark Sun Gwyndolin is going to continually warp away from you as you get close to him, so fighting at close range can be troublesome. Additionally, he will use Soul Spear and Homing Soul Mass, both of which will track you and hit you through walls. He also has a tracking ranged bow attack as well.
    • To make things easier for yourself, you are going to Poison him from a distance with the bow. This will take anywhere from 10-15 minutes while you wait for the Poison to kill him. You may supplement damage during this time with a great bow if you have one with ammo or upgraded (optional).
    • Once you enter the fog gate and the cutscene is over, move forward and to the left, 2 pillars as shown in the video. Dark Sun Gwyndolin will warp away and be non-aggro. From here, you can begin shooting him with Poison Arrows. Watch his health bar to confirm the Poison has started taking his health.
    • Do not move forward from this position as he will aggro and begin his ranged attacks. Repeat the shooting and application of Poison until he is dead.

    • Once defeated, he will drop the Soul of Gwyndolin and you will acquire  Defeat the Dark Sun Gwyndolin.
    • Continue on down the corridor for some items ahead.
    • Ahead are three chests in this giant tomb. The more important one is in the centre. Open it for Miracle: Sunlight Blade (21/23).
    • The chest on the right has a Brass Set and the left chest is empty. Return to the bonfire and warp to Anor Londo bonfire.
    • The Darkmoon Knightess will be hostile as you arrive, so kill her with any of your sorceries. She will drop a Fire Keeper Soul upon death.
    • The bonfire here is now inactive and cannot be used for the remainder of this playthrough. You won't be returning to this part of Anor Londo, so that's fine.
    • Warp to Daughter of Chaos to acquire another Fire Keeper Soul.
    • Kill Egg Carrier Eingyi, but beware of the Vile Maggots that appear when he dies. He drops 2 Egg Vermifuge.
    • Rest at the bonfire and kindle it twice. Once done, kill the Daughter of Chaos and loot the Fire Keeper Soul she drops.
    • Before leaving, loot the corpse in the hallway of this area for Thorns Set.
    • Walk to the Demon Ruins bonfire, kindle it three times and then warp to Firelink Shrine.
    • Speak to Anastacia of Astora and reinforce your Estus Flask+4 to Estus Flask+6.
    • This is the end of this portion of the playthrough.
